Word Scavenger — Rotate/Randomize interaction fix

Changes
- Rotate and Randomize buttons now disable whenever the board is in an action animation or any tile lock is active, including ordinary tile refills and Long Word Bonus transformations.
- Board pointer interaction is blocked while those locks/animations are active, then restored when the final lock clears.
- Rotate now pauses the round countdown for the duration of its five-batch flip animation and resumes it afterward if the timer was running.
- Existing clockwise 5x5 mapping, tile objects/bonus metadata, charge rules, and tile-flip animation were preserved.
- Randomize continues to reuse the existing short tile-flip animation; the selected tile's bonus type/color remains unchanged.

Validation
- JavaScript syntax checks: passed for js/game.js and js/theme-toggle.js.
- ZIP integrity: checked after packaging.
- Clockwise mapping: checked against a labeled 5x5 matrix.
- Browser interaction test: not completed. Headless Chromium did not finish loading the local game within the test timeout, so no claim is made that full interactive browser behavior passed.


2026-10-20 Rebalancing update
- Main timer cap increased to 200 seconds; base round remains 120 seconds.
- Updated bonus-tile time rewards and added additive Word Amplifier time rewards by word length.
- Word Amplifier multipliers now vary by exact word length: 3×1.0, 4×1.25, 5×1.5, 6×1.75, 7+×2.0.
- Chrono Combo now starts at 7 seconds and caps at 20 seconds; score progression unchanged.
- Bonus bag fixed to 500 entries: NO 350, 2LS 50, 3LS 40, 2WS 35, 3WS 25.
- Stored action caps increased to 10 Rotate charges and 5 Randomize charges; each Randomize use consumes one charge.

2026-10-21 — Timer cap and Bomb/Swap powers
- Added per-valid-word caps to combined main-timer rewards (base + bonus tiles + Word Amplifier): 10s for 3-letter words, 15s for 4, 20s for 5, 25s for 6, and 30s for 7+.
- Added Bomb charges (maximum 3), earned after 10 valid words or a qualifying 6-letter word. Bomb targets a 2×2 block by clicking the intersection of its four tiles; tiles above fall down two rows with their metadata preserved, and vacated upper positions receive random tiles.
- Added Swap charges (maximum 3), earned after 15 valid words or a qualifying 7-letter word. Swap mode selects two tiles and exchanges their complete tile data without score/time rewards.
- Charge awards are limited to one per power per accepted word, and stored charges never exceed their caps.
- Updated both themes with Bomb/Swap action styling.
- Static syntax and archive checks are required; browser interaction testing status is recorded separately in the delivery notes.

## 2026-10-21 — Bomb and Swap animation refinements
- Added a snapping 2×2 Bomb target preview that follows the pointer across valid board intersections.
- Reworked Bomb refill mapping so the two tiles immediately above the selected block fall into it, tiles above their former positions are newly generated, and tiles below the block stay unchanged.
- Added a localized explosion animation to only the four targeted tiles; unrelated tiles no longer use the flip animation during Bomb.
- Added position-based falling transitions for moved and newly generated Bomb tiles.
- Replaced Swap's tile flip with a short slide animation for the two exchanged tiles only.
- Preserved tile objects and their bonus metadata when moving existing tiles. Browser interaction testing remains required for visual approval.
