How to play

  1. Drag across letters to spell a word.
  2. Release to submit it. Valid words score points and the used tiles refill.
  3. Use bonus tiles for higher scores and extra time.
  4. Build long words to trigger the gold Word Amplifier.
  5. Find 5+ letter words to create extra bonus tiles: 5 letters grants 1, 6 letters grants 2, and 7+ letters grants up to 3.
  6. Keep a streak alive to earn blue-and-gold Chrono Combo rewards.
  7. Keep going until the timer reaches zero. Every valid word adds at least 2 seconds.
